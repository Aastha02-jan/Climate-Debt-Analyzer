# Climate-Debt-Analyzer

This project aims to analyze the environmental and economic patterns of the top 100 countries
over a 10-year period in relation to climate debt and sustainability performance. It leverages
the power of data science to explore the interconnected dynamics between national debt
associated with climate policies, carbon emissions, renewable energy investments, and
Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). The project seeks to provide a deeper understanding
of how financial decisions and environmental efforts collectively impact a country’s progress
toward sustainability targets.

To achieve this, machine learning algorithms, specifically regression models, have been used
to predict SDG scores using key features such as climate-related debt, CO₂ emissions, and
renewable energy spending. Furthermore, unsupervised learning techniques like clustering are
implemented to categorize countries based on similarities in environmental and economic
indicators. This approach enables the identification of patterns and outliers, facilitating
comparative analysis and more informed decision-making. The integration of interactive
dashboards and data visualization enhances the interpretability of results and empowers
policymakers with accessible insights for climate strategy formulation.
